## 1) Morin 3D, Design Engineering
**What I did:** owned design to release cycles for additive manufactured client hardware. I reverse engineered parts into parametric CAD, defined interfaces and serviceability, and shipped drawings, assemblies, BOMs, and tolerance notes.

**Impact story (fast):**
- Reduced iteration churn by making interfaces explicit and reviewable
- Improved manufacturability by designing around print constraints and fit checks
- Worked inside real client constraints and deadlines

**Open:** [case study page](case-studies/morin3d/README.md) | [brief](case-studies/morin3d/brief.pdf)

> Morin 3D delivers parts for external customers including Bird Construction, the Royal Canadian Navy, and more. Details are summarized in the case study with any sensitive info removed.

## 3) Guided Rocketry GNC, Simulation Harness to Hardware
**What I did:** built a simulation to hardware workflow under actuator and power constraints. Created a waypoint harness with randomized variation. Used explicit scoring metrics and traceable tuning logs.

**Why it matters:** this shows a verification mindset loop. Define a metric, simulate, vary conditions, accept or reject, update, retest.

**Open:** [case study page](case-studies/guided-gnc/README.md) | [brief](case-studies/guided-gnc/brief.pdf)
